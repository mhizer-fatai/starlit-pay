3. Information We Collect

Google User Data Compliance Notice:

We use your Google profile information solely to authenticate your identity and set up your account profile. B. Wallet and Cryptographic Data

Starlit Pay uses client-side cryptographic key derivation to safeguard user privacy:

  • Private Keys & PIN: We never collect, transmit, or store your private spending keys or your 6-digit payment PIN. All cryptographic operations occur client-side in your browser.
  • Public Addresses & Viewing Keys: We store your public Stellar deposit address, public encryption/viewing key, and encrypted transaction notes required to index incoming shielded payments.

C. Technical Information

We automatically collect standard diagnostic data when you interact with our servers, including browser user agent, IP address, device type, and server error logs to maintain security and reliability.

We use collected data solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To authenticate your session and protect against unauthorized account access.
  • To index and decrypt shielded payment notes on your authorized client devices.
  • To detect, prevent, and remediate technical errors or security incidents.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We implement multi-layered technical and organizational security measures, including:

  • End-to-end transport layer encryption (HTTPS/TLS) across all network communications.
  • Client-side symmetric encryption (NaCl Box) for all shielded note contents.
  • Secure database storage with row-level security policies and strict access controls.
